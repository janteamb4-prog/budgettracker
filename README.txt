BUDGET TRACKER\n\nFiles included:\n- index.html: website structure\n- style.css: liquid glass frontend design\n- script.js: existing budget calculations, adding/removing options, and browser autosave\n\nHow to use:\n1. Extract the ZIP file.\n2. Keep index.html, style.css, and script.js in the same folder.\n3. Open index.html in a modern browser.\n4. Enter your cutoff budget, rename expense options, and enter their amounts.\n\nData is saved in the browser's local storage on that device.\n
